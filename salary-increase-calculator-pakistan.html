How the Federal Salary Increase Is Calculated

The calculator matches your current running basic pay to the nearest official stage in the selected BPS-2022 scale. It then uses the corresponding point in BPS-2026 to estimate your revised basic pay.

The result separately shows the basic-pay difference, 7% ARA-2026 and the revised conveyance amount. DRA-2026 is included only when you select it and enter your actual basic pay as on 30 June 2022.

Federal and Provincial Employees

The BPS-2026 mode is based on federal Finance Division notifications for covered federal civil employees. Provincial governments, autonomous bodies, armed forces payroll and special cadres may issue separate implementation instructions.

Check the revised figure against your departmental payroll, AGPR record and applicable notification before making a financial decision.
