How the Federal Salary Increase Is Calculated

The selected BPS stage determines old and revised basic pay. The calculator then removes the discontinued ARA amount entered from the June payslip and adds the revised basic-pay difference, 7% ARA-2026, conveyance increase and any eligibility-based adjustments.

The June tax estimate annualizes June gross pay under the previous salary slabs. The July estimate annualizes revised gross pay under FY 2026-27 / Tax Year 2027 slabs. It is a comparison tool, not a payroll order.

Federal and Provincial Employees

The BPS-2026 mode is based on federal Finance Division notifications for covered federal civil employees. Provincial governments, autonomous bodies, armed forces payroll and special cadres may issue separate implementation instructions.

Check the revised figure against your departmental payroll, AGPR record and applicable notification before making a financial decision.
