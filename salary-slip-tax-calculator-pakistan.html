How to Read a Pakistani Salary Slip

Most salary-slip confusion happens because employees add every deduction together and call the whole amount “tax.” This page helps you separate the income tax line from other payroll items like provident fund, EOBI, loan recovery, unpaid leave, and company-specific adjustments.

That separation matters because salary tax follows annual slab logic, while many other deductions do not. A correct payslip review starts by identifying the tax line first and then evaluating the non-tax deductions separately.
