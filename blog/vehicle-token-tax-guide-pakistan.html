Islamabad Vehicle Token Tax 2026

PakTaxCalc Team • April 7, 2026

Vehicle tax depends on jurisdiction. This guide covers the explicit motor-vehicle tax table introduced by Finance Act 2026 for Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT); it is not a national replacement for provincial excise or registration charges.

The enacted ICT table is based on engine capacity and invoice value. It does not use the former ATL versus non-ATL or lump-sum rate card shown on older versions of this page.

Finance Act 2026 ICT Vehicle Tax Rates

  • Up to 1000cc: Rs. 20,000 lifetime tax.
  • 1001cc to 1300cc: 0.25% of invoice value per annum.
  • 1301cc to 1500cc: 0.25% of invoice value per annum.
  • 1501cc to 2000cc: 0.25% of invoice value per annum.
  • 2001cc to 2500cc: 0.35% of invoice value per annum.
  • 2501cc and above: 0.35% of invoice value per annum.

For a 1500cc vehicle with an invoice value of Rs. 4,000,000, the ICT annual tax estimate is Rs. 10,000. For a vehicle up to 1000cc, the Finance Act table instead specifies the Rs. 20,000 lifetime amount.

How To Check the Right Bracket

Do not guess from model names or dealership language. Token tax planning works best when you use the actual engine-capacity bracket shown in the registration record or vehicle documents. A 1299cc versus 1300cc difference may sound small in conversation, but on the rate table it changes the amount.

If you are unsure, verify the engine size first and only then estimate the tax.

Common Mistakes People Make

  • Mixing renewal token tax with registration, transfer, or customs cost
  • Using the wrong engine bracket based on informal model naming
  • Using annual figures in a lump-sum case
  • Ignoring ATL status when it materially changes the number
  • Relying on old screenshots instead of current published rate-card figures

Why Owners Get Confused Between Federal and Provincial Charges

Vehicle owners rarely encounter tax in neat legal categories. What they actually see is a stack of demands around ownership, registration, renewal, transfer, and compliance. Because those demands appear around the same asset, many people describe all of them as token tax. That is understandable, but it creates confusion when they try to estimate a current payment using the wrong number from the wrong stage of the ownership journey.

The practical fix is to separate the questions. If the issue is registration of a new vehicle, that is one cost conversation. If the issue is ownership transfer, that is another. If the issue is annual renewal or lump-sum renewal-stage tax for a private vehicle, section 234 is the right lens. Users make better decisions once they ask the narrower question first.

This is also why scope improves trust. A page that says exactly what it does not calculate is often more useful than one that pretends to calculate everything.

What Buyers Should Learn From Token Tax Before Buying a Car

Checklist Before You Trust a Token Tax Quote

Check the engine bracket from documents, not memory

People casually round engine size up or down in conversation. The official bracket is what matters, not how the market talks about the car.

Confirm whether the case is annual or lump sum

This single mistake can create the biggest difference in the final amount.

Do not ignore ATL status

Status is often the easiest thing to overlook and one of the biggest reasons a quoted amount feels wrong.

Use a current source, not an old screenshot

Vehicle tax screenshots travel for years on social media. The safest approach is always to tie the estimate back to the current published rate-card structure.
