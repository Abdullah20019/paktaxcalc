Salary After Tax in Pakistan 2026

PakTaxCalc Team•May 14, 2026•10 min read

People searching for salary after tax in Pakistan are not really asking about slabs alone. They want to know how much money will actually reach their bank account after income tax and normal payroll deductions. That is a take-home pay question, and it deserves its own dedicated answer.

Gross salary and take-home salary are not the same thing. In a normal payroll setup, your employer may deduct income tax, provident fund, EOBI, insurance, loan recovery, or other adjustments before salary is paid. That means even when two people have the same gross salary, they may still receive different after-tax amounts.

Quick Answer

To estimate salary after tax in Pakistan, annualize your salary, apply the annual salary tax slabs, divide annual tax by 12, and then subtract monthly tax plus payroll deductions from your gross monthly salary.

Why Take-Home Salary Matters More Than Gross Salary

Job offers are usually discussed in gross monthly terms, but budgeting happens in net monthly terms. If you do not estimate take-home salary properly, a package can look stronger on paper than it feels in practice. This is especially important when salary tax starts becoming visible around middle-income salary bands.

A good salary decision should always be based on what remains after tax and payroll deductions, not only on the headline gross number.

What Usually Reduces Salary After Tax

The main item is salary income tax. Beyond that, provident fund and EOBI are common deductions on Pakistani salary slips. Some companies also deduct loan repayments, leave adjustments, insurance, meals, transport recovery, or other payroll items.

That is why it is useful to separate tax deductions from non-tax deductions. You can do that with our salary slip tax calculator.

Why Bonus and Arrears Change Take-Home Pay

Bonus and arrears usually increase projected annual taxable income. Once that annual picture changes, payroll may revise the expected annual tax and recover the extra amount through the remaining salary months. That is why your after-tax salary can suddenly fall in months where bonus or backdated payments are processed.

If that happens, read our bonus guide and salary arrears guide.

A Practical Example

Suppose a person earns Rs. 100,000 per month. Their annual salary becomes Rs. 1.2 million. Under the current salary slab logic used on PakTaxCalc, that employee may see a modest monthly salary tax deduction, which then lowers take-home pay further when EOBI or provident fund is added.

That employee should not judge the package by Rs. 100,000 alone. The more useful number is the monthly amount left after tax and payroll deductions.

Key Takeaways

  • Salary after tax means net monthly salary after income tax and payroll deductions.
  • Gross salary should never be treated as the amount you will actually receive.
  • Provident fund, EOBI, bonus, and arrears can all change take-home pay.
  • Take-home salary is the right number to use for budgeting and job comparison.
