# AdSense readiness

The site has a working homepage, blog, contact page, About page, privacy policy,
terms and disclaimer. The current salary calculator shows its tax-year and payroll
assumptions; the About page links to FBR source material. The public privacy
policy explains that the AdSense verification tag is installed without claiming
that ads are already running. Publisher ID: `ca-pub-2426410046563968`.

Approval cannot be guaranteed by code changes. Before requesting review:

1. Review the published site on mobile and desktop, and verify every calculator
   and navigation link after deployment. Re-check salary rates against the enacted
   Finance Act and test the remaining non-salary tools separately.
2. Review each article for factual accuracy, original value, citations and
   duplication. Short, near-duplicate or outdated posts may need consolidation.
   Do not add words just to reach a target count.
   Priority review: `blog/atl-vs-non-atl-salaried-person-pakistan.html`,
   `blog/file-return-if-salaried-pakistan.html`,
   `blog/how-employer-calculates-salary-tax-pakistan.html`,
   `blog/income-tax-on-allowances-pakistan.html`,
   `blog/salary-after-tax-pakistan.html`,
   `blog/salary-arrears-tax-pakistan.html`,
   `blog/salary-tax-monthly-vs-annual-pakistan.html`, and
   `blog/tax-credits-for-salaried-persons-pakistan.html`.
3. Deploy the site, confirm the AdSense tag appears in the live page source,
   then use Verify in the AdSense account. Local files cannot verify ownership.
4. If AdSense requests `ads.txt`, use the exact seller line shown in that
   account. The publisher ID is known, but the seller relationship should not
   be guessed from the code snippet alone.
5. Before enabling ads for visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, configure a
   Google-certified consent management platform and update the privacy policy to
   reflect the actual ad configuration.
6. Check AdSense Sites and Policy Center after submission. Resolve any reported
   content, navigation or policy issue before requesting another review.
