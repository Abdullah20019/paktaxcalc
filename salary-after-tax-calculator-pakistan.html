How This Estimate Works

The estimator converts monthly gross salary to an annual figure, adds taxable bonus and applies FY 2026-27 salaried slabs. EOBI and provident-fund entries reduce the take-home estimate only; they are not automatically deducted from taxable salary.

It is designed for take-home pay planning, so the output emphasizes net monthly salary rather than only annual tax. That makes it useful when you are comparing job offers, checking a payslip, or planning an increment.
